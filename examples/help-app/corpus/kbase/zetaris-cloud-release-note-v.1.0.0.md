# Zetaris Cloud Release Note v.1.0.0

**The managed way to run the Zetaris Lightning data platform inside your own AWS account.** Your data stays in your cloud; we handle provisioning, autoscaling, monitoring, scheduling and billing.

At a glance

-   **Under 30 minutes** to a running cluster
-   **100%** of your data stays in your AWS account
-   **Free plan** to try — Hobby
-   **1-click** AWS account setup
-   **Managed compute clusters** — guided launch with Small / Medium / Large / Custom sizing, an autoscaling query engine, and reserve capacity, provisioned in under 30 minutes.
-   **Full lifecycle control** — suspend, resume, scale, terminate and reconnect, with live progress, an event timeline, and timezone-aware suspend/resume schedules.
-   **Bring your own cloud** — connect AWS with a one-click CloudFormation quickstart; least-privilege cross-account access, verified with a unique external ID, fully in your control.
-   **Usage-based billing** — metered by active vCPU-hours, live current-period spend, monthly invoices with per-cluster drill-down, plus Stripe payments, history and refunds.
-   **Teams & secure accounts** — self-service sign-up with email verification and phone-based MFA, email invitations, Owner/Admin/Member roles, and multi-organization switching.
-   **Secure & resilient** — hardware-secured, envelope-encrypted credentials; short-lived cross-account access; crash-safe provisioning with rollback; health heartbeat and full audit trails.

What ships in 1.0.0

* * *

Clusters run in your own AWS account — standard AWS resource charges apply and are billed by AWS separately from your Zetaris subscription. AWS is the supported deployment cloud in v1.0.0.
