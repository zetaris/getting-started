# How to connect to HADB

1.  Login to zetaris
    
2.  Click on the plus button next to virtual database source
    
     ![](https://kbase.zetaris.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-02-17-23-3888-AM.png)
    
3.  Click on next.And select HADB datasource.
    
     ![](https://kbase.zetaris.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-02-17-45-5279-AM.png)
    
4.  Then fill all the database related details. Then click on next.
    
5.  Tt will show all the tables in the database. slelect the ones that are needed to be register
    
6.  Then click next. Hadb will be registered.
    
    ![](https://kbase.zetaris.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-02-18-04-9510-AM.png)
