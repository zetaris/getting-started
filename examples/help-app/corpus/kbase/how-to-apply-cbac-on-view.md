# How to apply CBAC on View

1.  Login to zetaris
    
2.  Create a view using query builder
    
3.  Once view is created. Click on the three dots next to the view.then click on column access
    
     ![](https://kbase.zetaris.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-02-20-44-3028-AM.png)
    
4.  Under police editor , Click on “create policy”. then give name of the policy.
    
     ![](https://kbase.zetaris.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-02-21-03-5114-AM.png)
    
5.  Under Column, Select column name for which you want to change permission.
    
    ![](https://kbase.zetaris.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-02-21-29-8638-AM.png) 
    
6.  Policy will appear once you click on column name. change policy type to MASK. and click apply
    
     ![](https://kbase.zetaris.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-02-21-47-5263-AM.png)
    
7.  Once you click on Apply but will change to “update policy”. then click on update policy as well.
    
    ![](https://kbase.zetaris.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-02-22-19-2064-AM.png) 
    
8.  Policy is not created. Now policy needs to be assigned to a USER or ROLE.
    
9.  To do that open column access pop again.
    
10.  assign it to user or role.
    
     ![](https://kbase.zetaris.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-02-22-40-2729-AM.png)
    
11.  then again click on “update policy”. that will assign the policy to the user/role
