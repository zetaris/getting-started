# Accessing Zetaris Enterprise

Accessing Zetaris Enterprise

#### How to access Zetaris

• Use your custom Zetaris web URL to access the browser interface. Contact your Zetaris administrator if you are not sure what this is. Example: atlasbank.zetaris.com.

• If your organisation uses single sign-on, use your organisation's login URL instead, i.e. https://<your-zetaris-host>/<slug>/login. Your administrator can find it under **Management > Organisation**.

**To log in:**

1\. Enter your **Username** (your user email address).

2\. Enter your **Password**. Click the eye icon to show or hide it.

3\. Click **Sign In**.
