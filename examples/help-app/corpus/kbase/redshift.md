# AWS Redshift

## This article covers how to connect Zetaris to AWS Redshift.

### Setup

1.  Login to your Zetaris Environment
2.  Click on **NDP Fabric Builder   
    ![redshift0](https://kbase.zetaris.com/hs-fs/hubfs/redshift0.png)** 
3.  Click on the yellow create button next to **Virtual Database Sources** as shown below  
    ![redshift1](https://kbase.zetaris.com/hs-fs/hubfs/redshift1.png)
4.  A Popup window will appear. Click **'Next'**  
    ![redshift2](https://kbase.zetaris.com/hs-fs/hubfs/redshift2.png)
5.  Specify the following values and click **Next**

6.  Select the Tables you want to add to Zetaris. and then Click **Next**  
    ![redshift4](https://kbase.zetaris.com/hs-fs/hubfs/redshift4.png)
7.  Review your Inputs and then Click on '**Register**'
8.  You will see your newly created Redshift data source now.  
    ![redshift6](https://kbase.zetaris.com/hs-fs/hubfs/redshift6.png)
9.  To view the contents of your table click on the preview button  ![preview](https://kbase.zetaris.com/hs-fs/hubfs/preview.png)  
    ![redshift7](https://kbase.zetaris.com/hs-fs/hubfs/redshift7.png)
