## 1. Why a separate plan from `edgar-sic-enrichment-plan.md`

That plan's scope was "build the EDGAR+SIC data product using Zetaris's composition primitives available at the time" — which turned out to be `SCHEMASTORE VIEW` + a manual, SQL-free Virtual Data Mart (section 5.3 of that plan: confirmed no SQL surface exists for VDM). USL is a different primitive, introduced to this repo after that plan closed, and per `docs/guides/zetaris-sql-companion.md` section 7-8, it appears to close exactly the gap that plan had to accept as a permanent manual step. That's a distinct enough claim — "USL can fully script what VDM couldn't" — that it deserves its own build-and-verify plan rather than being folded into the closed-out original, especially since the original plan's own file should stay a historical record of what was actually built and tested at the time, not be rewritten around a primitive that didn't exist yet when it was written.

2. **Materialization is deferred, not answered.** No cloud storage target is configured on any instance this repo has touched, and standing one up is out of scope right now. V4 and V7 stay in the checklist (so the gap doesn't get silently forgotten) but are explicitly marked deferred; every other step is written to not depend on them, so the rest of the plan can complete without a storage target existing.
3. **A human runs section 4.** Michael or a colleague executes these steps directly against a live Zetaris SQL Workspace — this repo/session has no such access itself. The expected handoff: whoever runs a step reports back what happened (the actual output, error text, timing, or screenshot — not just pass/fail), and that gets written into this plan's checklist (section 4) and into `docs/guides/zetaris-sql-companion.md` section 8 per the feedback loop in section 6 above. Until that reporting happens, nothing in section 4 should be treated as more than "written, not run" (section 5).
